About Black Veil Brides

Black Veil Brides is an American rock band formed in 2006. Known for their unique style, blending glam metal, hard rock, and post-hardcore, they have inspired a massive fanbase worldwide. Their signature black attire, makeup, and theatrical performances set them apart.

The band has released multiple albums including "We Stitch These Wounds", "Set the World on Fire", and "Vale". With powerful lyrics, epic riffs, and anthemic choruses, BVB continues to dominate the rock scene and touch the hearts of fans globally.
